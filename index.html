Introducing Shruti Rajkumar

Shruti Rajkumar is a journalist and a student at the Craig Nemwark Graduate School of Journalism at CUNY. Previously, they were a breaking news reporter for HuffPost. Rajkumar has a beat in disability reporting and has experience covering general assignment.

  1. Disabled Lives Are An Afterthought During School Shootings. But Why? - A lot of people don't know what the protocols are for disabled students, and are shockd when they learn that the protocol is to leave them behind. I was proud to educate people on this.
  2. Advocates Fear The Impact Of NYC’s Involuntary Hospitalization Plan - I loved writing this piece because I got to connect this NYC plan to the Ugly Laws, which not a lot of people know about because disability history isn't widely taught in U.S. schools.
  3. The ADA was a victory for the disabled community, but we need more. My life shows why - I loved this article because I not only got to turn a case of discrimination into something positive and beautiful, but I also got to interview the mother of the disability movement before she died.
